We're Moving to Valley View Elementary!

Austin Chinese Church Southwest is preparing to transition its Sunday worship location from Cedar Creek Elementary to Valley View Elementary nearby.

Confirmed Move Date

We are currently finalizing the transition timeline with Eanes ISD. Sunday worship and Sunday school will continue at Cedar Creek Elementary as usual until the official date is confirmed.

To Be Announced
