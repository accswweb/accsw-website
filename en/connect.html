PROGRAMS & MINISTRIES

Connect with Us

We strive to be a healthy church congregation that joyfully grows together to learn how to follow Jesus in every aspect of our lives.

Sunday Mornings

10 AM

First Hour Programs

Children’s Program

Our Children’s Program on Sundays has fun, interactive and biblically-based programming for children from preschool to 5th grade. The program continues from first hour through to the end of the second hour worship service.

Youth Ministry

Our Youth Ministry brings middle school and high school youth into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ through fellowship led by our counselors.

Adult Bible Fellowship

Our Adult Bible Fellowships offer the opportunity to mature spiritually through practical and relevant teaching on God’s Word.

11:15 AM

Second Hour Service

The worship service at ACC Southwest begins with a time of worshipping using Chinese and English bilingual songs that we invite all adults, youth, and children to come join us in singing. Following worship, we will have an English sermon and Chinese sermon.

Fellowships & Ministries

Since ACC Southwest is mainly an additional site for Sunday worship, people who attend Sunday worship at ACC Southwest are invited to join ACC’s many different programs on other days.

Adult Fellowship Groups

The fellowship groups listed below are specifically connected with ACC Southwest:

  • English Missional Group
  • English Discovery Group (for those seeking Christ)
  • Hill Country Fellowship (Bee Cave area, Mandarin group + English group, Saturday evening)
  • Faith-Hope-Love Home Group (Circle C area, Mandarin group, Saturday evening)
  • South Family Fellowship (Mandarin group, Saturday evening)
  • Biao-Gan Home Group (Mandarin group, Friday evening)
  • Senna Hills Sisters Home Group (Mandarin group, Thursday morning)

ACC has additional Cantonese-speaking, English-speaking and Mandarin-speaking fellowship groups that meet at a variety of different locations and times.
