Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our upcoming move.

Where do we meet this coming Sunday?
We continue to meet every Sunday at Cedar Creek Elementary (3301 Pinnacle Rd) until the confirmed move date is officially announced. All service times and programs remain exactly the same.
When will the confirmed move date be known?
The move date is currently to be announced. We are coordinating the final paperwork and logistics with Eanes ISD. Once the date is confirmed, we will post it here, announce it in the bulletin, and share it during Sunday announcements.
Will children and youth programs continue as normal?
Yes. Both the Children’s Program and Youth Ministry will continue seamlessly at the new campus. Valley View Elementary provides great classroom and recreational spaces for our young people to learn and grow in faith.
How can I help with the moving process?
We will need volunteers for equipment packing, transport, setup, welcoming new visitors, and wayfinding signage. If you would like to lend a hand, please speak with church leaders or reach out through our connect page.
